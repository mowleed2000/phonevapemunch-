Phone Charging Port Repair

Loose charger cables, slow charging, or completely dead USB-C / Lightning ports.

Call Phone Vape and Munch

What's Involved

A worn-out charging port makes recharging your device frustrating or impossible. We clean packed lint from loose ports or replace the entire charging jack assembly to restore full power connection and fast charging speeds.

Signs You Need This Service

  • Charging cable feels loose and easily falls out of the port
  • Device only charges when the cable is wiggled or held at an angle
  • Phone completely fails to charge or register connection when plugged in
  • Extremely slow charging times compared to normal speeds
