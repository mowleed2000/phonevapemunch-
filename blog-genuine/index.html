Everything Genuine: Why It Matters

Published June 10, 2026 by Sammy Ahmadi

For 25 years, Phone Vape and Munch has stood on Richmond High Street as a trusted fixture for local residents. In an industry flooded with cheap knock-offs, third-party copy screens, and sub-standard toners, we have maintained a strict policy: Everything Genuine. Here is why using authentic parts and materials is critical to the longevity of your devices and documents.

Why Cheap Repair Screens Fail

Many repair shops offer lower prices by using generic copies of LCD screens or batteries. While cheap screens might look okay initially, they suffer from:

  • Ghost Touch & Lag: Non-genuine screens often lag, fail to register multi-touch gestures, or type random keys on their own.
  • Fast Battery Drain: Third-party screens often draw up to 30% more power, draining your phone's battery in hours.
  • Bricked Systems: Apple and Samsung regularly release software updates that can completely lock or disable devices containing non-genuine digitizers.

Authentic Printing Materials & Toners

Our commitment to quality extends to our print shop. We use genuine high-yield toner cartridges and heavy, premium paper stocks for all A4, A5, and A6 print outputs. Generic ink cartridges leak, cause smudges, and fade when exposed to light, whereas our genuine prints remain sharp and readable for years.
